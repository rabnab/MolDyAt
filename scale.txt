Reduced (LJ) units used by the simulation, and their SI conversion.
Reference substance: argon. Valid for noble-gas-like particles only.
The constants live in core/src/application/sim_stats.rs; keep this file in sync.

Pair potential: U(r) = 4 eps ((sigma/r)^12 - (sigma/r)^6), r = centre distance
  sigma = zero crossing of U, equilibrium (minimum) at r_m = 2^(1/6) sigma = 1.1225 sigma

Reduced units (all internal quantities, marked *):
  sigma* = 1        length   sigma = 3.405E-10 m
  eps*   = 1        energy   eps   = 122 kB = 1.684E-21 J
  m*     = 1        mass     m     = 39.948 u = 6.63E-26 kg  (Ar)
  kB*    = 1        temperature  T = eps/kB = 122 K

Conversion (X* = reduced value):
  L [m]   = L* sigma
  E [J]   = E* eps
  T [K]   = T* eps/kB
  M [kg]  = M* m
  t [s]   = t* tau,  tau = sigma sqrt(m/eps) = 2.137E-12 s (2.156E-12 s in the code)
  v [m/s] = v* sigma/tau
  a [m/s^2] = a* sigma/tau^2   (gravity: a* = g tau^2/sigma)

Temperature in reduced units (2D, kB* = 1):
  T* = 1/N sum_i E_kin,i*     (E_kin* = 1/2 m* v*^2, 2 degrees of freedom per ball)

Equation of motion in reduced units has no free constants:
  m* d2x*/dt*^2 = F*,   F* = -dU*/dx*
  Derivation: F = -dU/dx = (eps/sigma) F*  and  m a = m (sigma/tau^2) m* a*
  with tau^2 = m sigma^2/eps  ==>  F* = m* a*.

Other species, expressed in the argon units above (sigma* = sigma/3.405 A,
eps* = (eps/kB)/122 K, m* = m/39.948 u). Single-site LJ approximations;
values are typical literature numbers, rounded. Check them against a cited
source before relying on them. The code currently has sigma* = eps* = m* = 1
for every ball (species are planned, see docs/refactoring-plan.md I3/I5).

  species  sigma [A]  eps/kB [K]  m [u]    sigma*  eps*   m*     tau [ps]
  Ar       3.405      122         39.948   1.000   1.000  1.000  2.14
  N2       3.70       95          28.014   1.087   0.779  0.701  2.20
  H2       2.915      38.0        2.016    0.856   0.311  0.050  0.74
  H2O (*)  3.166      78.2        18.015   0.930   0.641  0.451  1.67
  Au (**)  2.629      2662        196.967  0.772   21.82  4.931  0.78

  tau column = sigma sqrt(m/eps) of that species. In argon units the same
  timescale ratio is sigma* sqrt(m*/eps*) (N2 1.03, H2 0.35, H2O 0.78, Au 0.37),
  so light/stiff species need a smaller dt in argon units.

  (*) O-site of SPC/E water, whole-molecule mass. LJ alone is NOT water:
      it needs charges (hydrogen bonds) and rigid-molecule geometry.
  (**) Heinz et al. 12-6 fit (eps = 5.29 kcal/mol, r_m = 2.951 A,
       sigma = r_m / 2^(1/6)). Metals are many-body (EAM-like); LJ only
       gives a rough cohesive/lattice model.
  H2: quantum effects matter below ~100 K; classical LJ is approximate.
